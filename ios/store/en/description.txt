Let Go is a small, quiet place for the thought that's weighing on you.

Write it down. Tap "Let go". Each word slips into dark water, one at a time, with a soft ripple, a gentle note and a light tap you can feel. When the last word has gone, the app walks you through one slow breath.

That's all. No accounts, no streaks, no reminders.

• Nothing you write is saved or sent anywhere. It stays for a moment, then it's gone.
• Works offline. No ads, no tracking, no data collected.
• Calm sound generated on the fly. It follows your silent switch and can be turned off.
• Respects Reduce Motion.
• In English and Italian.

Just a pause: a minute to put a heavy thought down.
